    How captures work

    • Moving onto an enemy piece opens a question. Right answer: the capture happens.
    • Wrong answer or time out: your piece stays put and you lose your turn.
    • Bigger targets, harder questions. Pawn is easy, knight and bishop are medium, rook and queen are hard.
    • Kings capture for free. No question when the king does the taking.
    • Miss a capture while your king is in check and your king falls.
    • The computer is Stockfish at the strength you pick. It answers questions too: easy 80% of the time, medium 65%, hard 50%.
